## Who this is for

Career changers, fresh graduates, and anyone who has been testing for a while but never had a structured foundation.

## How we teach it

You join a simulated sprint team. Every week you receive user stories for a real application, write test cases, execute them, log bugs in Jira and demo your findings. By week five you have done five sprints' worth of actual QA work.

## Syllabus

| Week | Topic |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | What testing is for. SDLC, STLC, test levels and types |
| 2 | Test design: equivalence partitioning, boundaries, decision tables, state transitions |
| 3 | Agile and Scrum in practice. Jira, TestRail, writing stories and acceptance criteria |
| 4 | Bugs that get fixed: reporting, severity vs priority, triage, DevTools and proxies |
| 5 | Mobile, cross-browser and accessibility basics. Resume and interview prep |
